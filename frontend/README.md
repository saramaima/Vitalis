# Vitality Tracker

Prompt:

Upgrade this Health & Nutrition Tracking web app to a more professional, premium SaaS look, matching this reference direction:

Landing page

Full marketing homepage with navbar (Home, Features, About, Contact, Login, Sign Up button)

Hero section: bold headline ("Track your health. Transform your life."), subtext, two CTA buttons (primary + secondary), and a large hero image of a healthy meal bowl with floating stat cards overlaid on top (e.g. "Today's Calories 1450/2000", "Water Intake 5/8 glasses")

Row of 5 feature icons with short labels below the hero (Meal Tracking, Exercise Tracking, Water Tracking, Weight Tracking, Progress Insights)

A full-width lifestyle photo section with a dark overlay panel containing a headline ("Everything you need to achieve your goals") and supporting text

Dashboard

Left sidebar with logo, nav icons (Dashboard, Meals, Exercise, Water, Weight, Progress, Goals, Settings, Log out), active item highlighted in soft green

Top bar: page title, welcome message with user's name, date picker, notification bell, user avatar

Row of 4 stat cards (Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat) each with a progress bar and percentage

Three-card row: Water Intake (glass icons), Exercise (calories burned + duration), Current Weight (mini line chart with trend arrow)

Two-column section: Today's Meals list (with thumbnails, times, calories) + a circular donut chart Nutrition Summary breaking down protein/carbs/fat/others with a legend

Meals page

Tabs for Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snacks

Each food item as a row with a thumbnail image, serving size, macro breakdown, and calories

Running total card at the top and bottom of each meal section

"+ Add Food" button in the header

Exercise & Water quick-add

Modal dialogs (not full pages) triggered from Quick Actions, with a relevant photo/illustration on one side and a clean form on the other (dropdowns, number inputs, date picker, large save button)

Progress page

Time-range tabs (7 Days / 30 Days / 3 Months)

Large weight trend line chart with the current value called out

Three summary stat cards below (Average Calories, Total Workouts, Water Average)

Onboarding

Multi-step form with a numbered progress stepper at the top (1 About You → 2 Your Goal → 3 Targets)

Split layout: form fields on one side, lifestyle photo with props (dumbbells, water bottle) on the other

Settings

Tabbed layout (Profile / Goals / Targets / Preferences)

Profile photo upload, editable fields in a clean two-column grid

Design system

Primary: soft green (#2E7D5B-ish), white backgrounds, soft shadows, rounded corners (12–16px)

Use real food/lifestyle photography, not just icons, to make it feel premium

Consistent card padding, clear typography hierarchy, subtle hover states

Fully responsive: sidebar collapses to bottom nav on mobile
